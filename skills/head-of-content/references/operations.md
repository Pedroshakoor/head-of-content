# Content operations and hiring

## Pipeline

`Backlog → Research → Angle approved → Draft → Design/edit → QA → Final approval → Scheduled → Published → Measured → Archived/repurposed`

Every content card: `ID | pillar | ICP | insight | platform | hook | references | brief | owner | due | reviewer | status | URL | CTA/tracking | results | next iteration`.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: review results; rank 3–5 experiments by impact, confidence, effort.
- Tuesday: research, concepts, creative packaging and concept approval.
- Wednesday–Thursday: produce, edit, verify claims and perform brand/accessibility QA.
- Friday: distribute, engage and capture audience signals, retrospective.

Scale cadence to actual team capacity. Coordinate distributed teams with written handoffs.

## Responsibility

- Content lead: positioning, priorities, creative quality, KPI interpretation and decision making.
- Writer: insight research, source checking, copy and revisions.
- Designer/editor: visual quality, platform execution, first-frame/thumbnail.
- Community/distribution: posts, responses, collaborations, owned opt-ins.
- Founder/client: real product evidence, context and necessary approvals.

A lean team may combine roles, never accountability.

## SOP template

`Purpose | trigger | owner | inputs | exact steps | QA criteria | approval | output location | expected cycle | metric | common failures | revision history`.

Start with simple Docs, task boards and shared folders before layering elaborate tools.

## Hiring and paid trials

Assess independent reasoning, audience understanding, taste and packaging, demonstrated results with caveats, platform fluency, leadership and reliability. Follower count alone is not skill.

**Compensated timed assignment:**
1. Give each candidate the same product brief, audience, constraints, 3 real assets and baseline analytics.
2. Request a positioning diagnosis, five original angles, three materially different packages for one idea, a production-ready brief and a test plan.
3. Ask what they would refuse to publish, and why.
4. Score each from 1–5: insight 25%, audience specificity 20%, packaging 20%, feasibility 15%, analysis 10%, communication 10%.
5. Use the same review criteria and ask candidates to reason through feedback.

Compensation can combine steady income, decision authority, realistic staffing, measurable bonuses, support for the creator's own brand and (where appropriate) formal equity agreements.
