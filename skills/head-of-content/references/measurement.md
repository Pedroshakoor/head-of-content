# Measurement, audits and experiments

## KPI hierarchy

1. **Business outcomes:** attributable or assisted revenue, qualified leads, demo bookings, activated users, engaged subscribers, retained customers.
2. **Audience fit:** qualified follows, relevant profile visits, relevant replies, ICP community joins, lead-to-customer conversion.
3. **Content diagnostics:** qualified reach, views, watch time, retention, saves, shares, click-through, hold, completion.
4. **Operations:** turnaround time, producer hours, cost per qualified result.

Never treat untracked referrals as precisely attributable sales.

## Formulas (only where denominators exist)

- Early hold rate = views remaining at specified timestamp / video starts.
- CTR = tracked clicks / eligible impressions (define eligibility).
- Landing conversion = completed actions / landing visits.
- Qualified-lead rate = qualified leads / total leads.
- Subscriber conversion = confirmed subscribers / attributable visits.
- Cost efficiency = qualified outcomes / production cost (state methodology).

Native platforms may define metrics differently; do not silently compare incompatible denominators.

## Diagnostic hypotheses

- High impressions + weak early hold: opening, promise mismatch, or targeting.
- Good retention + low relevant reach: distribution, specificity, sample size, platform fit.
- High CTR + low landing conversion: landing offer/page mismatch, trust, speed, audience quality.
- Engagement + low qualified actions: wrong audience, inappropriate CTA, offer or funnel stage.
- Consistent weakness: question ICP and underlying insights, not just packaging.

Treat these as hypotheses, not proven causes.
