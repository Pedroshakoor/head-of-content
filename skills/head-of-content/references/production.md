## Distinct hook patterns

1. Specific unexpected discovery with evidence.
2. Credible costly mistake with a real takeaway.
3. Contradiction that challenges a common assumption and resolves it.
4. Immediate demonstration of the payoff.
5. Real story in progress with obstacle and stakes.

Avoid unearned "you won't believe", unverifiable wealth claims, and empty curiosity gaps.

## X post / thread

`audience | insight | first-line hook | body (one thought per paragraph) | verifiable proof / link | CTA if needed | character limit`

Each thread entry should reward reading. Opening post must make sense alone. Write like a person, not a motivational template; do not force emoji, hashtags or em-dashes.

## Screenshot carousel: 3–6 slides

| Slide | Purpose | Overlay | Asset | Transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Specific problem or result | 3–12 legible words | strongest relevant screenshot | question / tension |
| 2 | Evidence / mechanism | precise callout | real screenshot | show how |
| 3 | Practical step / decision | direct instruction | UI/setup/example | payoff |
| 4–5 | Optional caveat or additional proof | concrete message | real asset | resolution |
| Last | Takeaway / action | believable CTA | relevant screenshot or neutral background | closure |

Do not claim a chart proves profitability or a backtested win rate. With only chart images, explain what they actually show rather than inventing trades or payouts.

## Short video

`platform | length | ratio | visual opening (0–2 sec) | first-line hook | beat-by-beat actions | proof/demo | payoff | caption | CTA | editing/audio | assets | constraints`

Optimize for clear first frame and early relevance. If the brief specifies a fixed webcam/phone shot, use character movement and timing for energy; never invent camera movement or edits.

## Preflight QA

- Does the idea matter to a specific audience?
- Is the opening credible and arresting?
- Can evidence be shown, rather than merely claimed?
- Is the payoff worth the viewer's time?
- Are overlays legible and nonrepetitive?
- Can a producer execute with available assets/budget?
- Would this still be worth sharing if viewers knew AI was involved?
